# Brightness and Contrast Explained

## Brightness Basics

### Brightness Unit: nits (cd/mÂ²)
**Definition:** Number of candelas per square meter

### Common Brightness Reference Values
- **Indoor Lighting:** 50-100 nits
- **Standard Monitor:** 250-400 nits
- **High Brightness Monitor:** 600-1000+ nits
- **Direct Sunlight:** 100,000+ nits
- **Human Eye Adaptation Range:** 0.01-100,000 nits

## ª Contrast Principle

### Contrast Definition
**Formula:** Contrast = Brightest White Ã Darkest Black

### Contrast Types

#### Static Contrast
- **Definition:** Ratio of brightest and darkest pixels shown simultaneously
- **Significance:** Reflects true on-screen contrast capability
- **Reference Values:** 1000:1 (standard LCD) to ž:1 (OLED)

#### Dynamic Contrast
- **Definition:** Ratio of brightest and darkest images at different times
- **Implementation:** By adjusting backlight brightness
- **Problem:** Inflated values, limited practical significance

## Panel Technology Contrast Characteristics

### IPS Panel
- **Static Contrast:** 1000:1 - 1200:1
- **Characteristics:** Relatively low contrast
- **Black Performance:** Slightly grayish
- **Advantages:** Accurate colors, good viewing angles

### VA Panel
- **Static Contrast:** 3000:1 - 6000:1
- **Characteristics:** Highest contrast among LCD panels
- **Black Performance:** Relatively deep
- **Advantages:** Good entertainment experience

### TN Panel
- **Static Contrast:** 600:1 - 1000:1
- **Characteristics:** Low contrast
- **Black Performance:** Obviously grayish
- **Advantages:** Fast response speed

### OLED
- **Static Contrast:** Theoretically infinite
- **Characteristics:** Self-illuminating pixels
- **Black Performance:** True black
- **Advantages:** Best contrast

## Impact of Backlight Technology on Monitor Brightness and Contrast

### Traditional LED Backlight
- **Structure:** Edge LED + light guide plate
- **Brightness:** 250-400 nits
- **Contrast:** Affected by backlight bleeding
- **Cost:** Lowest

### Direct LED Backlight
- **Structure:** LED array direct illumination
- **Brightness:** 300-600 nits
- **Contrast:** Slightly better than edge-lit
- **Uniformity:** May have hotspots

### Local Dimming
- **Structure:** Independently controlled LED zones
- **Brightness:** 600-1000+ nits
- **Contrast:** Significantly improved
- **Cost:** Higher

### MiniLED Backlight
- **Structure:** Extremely small LED beads
- **Brightness:** 1000+ nits
- **Contrast:** Close to OLED
- **Cost:** Expensive

## Common Misconceptions

### "Higher Contrast Numbers Are Always Better"
- **Problem:** Dynamic contrast numbers are inflated
- **Reality:** Static contrast is more important
- **Advice:** Focus on static contrast specifications

### "Higher Brightness Is Always Better"
- **Problem:** Excessive brightness hurts eyes
- **Reality:** Brightness suitable for environment is best
- **Advice:** Choose based on usage environment

### "OLED Contrast Is Infinite"
- **Problem:** Theoretical values don't equal perception
- **Reality:** Affected by ambient light
- **Advice:** Experience in actual environment
