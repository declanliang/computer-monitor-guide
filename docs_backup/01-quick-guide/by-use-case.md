# Choose Display by Use Case

## 🖥️ Daily Office & Study

**Core Requirements:** Comfortable for long-term use, clear text, reasonable price

### Recommended Specifications:
- **Size:** 24-27 inches
- **Resolution:** 1080P (24") / 2K (27")
- **Panel:** IPS (accurate colors, good viewing angles)
- **Refresh Rate:** 60-75Hz (sufficient)
- **Special Features:** Blue light filter, height-adjustable stand

### Why Choose This?
- **24-27 inches:** Fits common desk depth, won't cause visual fatigue
- **IPS Panel:** No color shift, more comfortable for long work sessions
- **60Hz is enough:** Office software doesn't require high refresh rates

---

## 🎮 Gaming & Entertainment

**Core Requirements:** Smooth gaming experience, fast response, immersive feel

### Competitive Games (CS, LOL, etc.)
- **Size:** 24-27 inches
- **Resolution:** 1080P-2K
- **Refresh Rate:** 144Hz+
- **Response Time:** Under 1ms
- **Panel:** TN/Fast IPS
- **Sync Technology:** G-Sync/FreeSync

### Single-player AAA Games
- **Size:** 27-32 inches
- **Resolution:** 2K-4K
- **Refresh Rate:** 60-144Hz
- **Panel:** IPS/VA
- **Special Features:** HDR support

### Why Choose This?
- **High Refresh Rate:** Reduces motion blur, provides smoother visual experience
- **Low Response Time:** Reduces ghosting, crucial in fast-paced games
- **Sync Technology:** Eliminates screen tearing, provides smooth gaming experience

---

## 🎨 Design & Creation

**Core Requirements:** Color accuracy, high resolution, large workspace

### Recommended Specifications:
- **Size:** 27-32 inches
- **Resolution:** 2K-4K
- **Panel:** Professional IPS
- **Color Gamut:** 99% sRGB / Adobe RGB
- **Color Accuracy:** ΔE < 2
- **Special Features:** Hardware calibration, preset color modes

### Why Choose This?
- **High Resolution:** Shows more details, reduces zoom operations
- **Wide Color Gamut:** Displays richer colors, closer to print effects
- **Color Accuracy:** Ensures authenticity of design work colors

---

## 📺 Video & Audio Entertainment

**Core Requirements:** Large screen, excellent colors, comfortable viewing experience

### Recommended Specifications:
- **Size:** 32 inches+
- **Resolution:** 4K
- **Panel:** VA (high contrast) / IPS
- **Special Features:** HDR support, eye protection mode
- **Refresh Rate:** 60Hz (sufficient)

### Why Choose This?
- **Large Size:** Provides immersive viewing experience
- **4K Resolution:** Matches modern video content
- **VA Panel:** Deep black performance, suitable for movie watching
- **HDR:** Richer brightness levels and color performance

---

## 💡 Key Selection Points

1. **Define Primary Use:** Don't be misled by "all-in-one" products
2. **Reasonable Budget Allocation:** Spend money on the most important parameters
3. **Consider Usage Environment:** Desk size, lighting conditions, etc.
4. **Avoid Over-configuration:** Configurations beyond needs are wasteful
